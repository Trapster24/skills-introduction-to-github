## 2. Company background

- **The founder's track record:** Alex Mashrabov co-founded AI Factory, the computer-vision startup behind Snapchat's Cameos and face-filter technology. Snap bought it for about **$166M** (reported as 2019 or 2020). He then spent several years at Snap as Head/Director of Generative AI before leaving to start Higgsfield in 2023.
- **Roots:** The company is often described as a Kazakh-founded startup (both founders have Kazakh links), headquartered in San Francisco.
- **Its place in the market:** Higgsfield is mainly a **creative layer and aggregator**, not a foundation-model lab. Its value lies in combining the best available models with production-style tools: camera controls, character consistency, presets and a studio workflow.

## 4. Business model (in plain English)

1. **Subscription plus credits.** Users pay a monthly or annual plan and receive a pool of credits. Each generation costs credits, and the cost depends on the model, clip length and resolution.
   - Plan tiers (these change often, so check before filming): **Free → Starter (~$15–19/mo) → Plus (~$49–59/mo) → Ultra (~$129/mo) → Business/Team.**
   - Rough credit costs: **Kling 3.0 ≈ 7–10 credits per 5s at 720p. Seedance 2.0 ≈ 22–23 credits per 5s at 720p** (about 45 at 1080p).
   - Promotions such as "7-day unlimited Kling" windows on higher tiers come and go.
2. **Model aggregation.** Higgsfield licenses access to leading models (ByteDance's **Seedance**, Kuaishou's **Kling**, Google's **Veo** and others) and wraps them in its own tooling. It makes money on the gap between what it charges in credits and what it pays for compute and API access.
3. **Own tools as the differentiator:** Cinema Studio (shot and camera direction, now into post-production), Soul / Soul ID / Soul Cast (consistent characters and identities), Lip-Sync Studio, Audio, Click-to-Ad / Marketing Studio, Canvas, and an API.
4. **Business and brand customers.** Team/Business plans target agencies and brands. The CEO has claimed widespread Fortune 500 use for big ad campaigns; this is a company claim.
5. **Creator ecosystem.** A creator fund and "Higgsfield Earn" clipping programme pay creators to make and post content. This drives a lot of its organic marketing.

**On "turnover":** there is no published turnover figure. The figure you can safely use is the **reported annualised revenue run-rate of about $1 billion (September 2026)**. That is current monthly revenue × 12, as reported by the press, not audited annual turnover.

## 5. Seedance and Kling on Higgsfield (for your video)

- **Kling 3.0** (Kuaishou, released 5 Feb 2026). Higgsfield had **temporary exclusive access** at launch. It is the cheaper, fast model, well suited to iteration and high volume.
- **Seedance 2.0** (ByteDance, Feb 2026). It came to Higgsfield around **5 April 2026** (Business plan first, then all plans from 9 April). It offers joint audio and video generation, multi-shot narratives and strong character consistency. It is the premium-quality option and costs more credits. **Seedance 2.5** has also been reported on the platform (30-second single takes, many reference images, native 1080p). Confirm what you see in the app before you quote it.
- **The useful angle for viewers:** one subscription and one interface give you both models side by side, so you can draft cheaply in Kling and do final hero shots in Seedance.

## 6. Things you should know (for your judgement, not for the script)

This section exists so you don't get caught out in the comments. Look it over before you finalise anything.

- **Billing and "unlimited" plan complaints (late 2025 to early 2026).** Forbes, The Register, Dealroom and others reported user complaints about "unlimited" plans later being restricted or throttled, difficult cancellations, unexpected annual charges and refunds refused after use. The company said it was clarifying its billing policies and issued some refunds.
- **Forbes exposé (Feb 2026).** It alleged demo footage that was partly stock footage, non-consensual celebrity deepfakes and offensive content circulating from its marketing and clipping programme. Higgsfield said it was strengthening moderation.
- **X account suspended (~9 Feb 2026)** after the Higgsfield Earn clipping push. The CEO said X gave no explanation.
- **Backlash over a deleted post** claiming its AI had "replaced dozens of VFX artists".
- **Influencer payment complaints.** Some creators on earlier sponsored campaigns said they were paid much less than expected, or nothing. **This applies directly to you:** get your fee, deliverables and payment date in writing, and ideally get paid upfront or on delivery.

You don't need to mention any of this in the video. But don't make claims that contradict it, such as "totally unlimited" or "cancel any time, hassle-free", unless you've checked them yourself on your own account.

## 7. Staying fair without being negative: the ground rules

1. **Only state what you've tested or what's sourced.** Say "reportedly" or "according to reports" for funding, revenue and user numbers.
2. **Describe pricing accurately.** It is credit-based, Seedance uses more credits than Kling, and viewers should check the current plan page.
3. **Avoid absolute claims** such as "best", "unlimited", "no catch" or "every Fortune 500 company".
4. **UK disclosure is mandatory.** Under ASA/CAP and CMA rules, a paid sponsorship must be clearly labelled **"Ad"** (or "Advertisement") **upfront**, in the video and in the description. On YouTube, also tick **"includes paid promotion"**. Words like "thanks to Higgsfield for supporting the channel" on their own are **not** enough.
5. **Editorial control.** If Higgsfield wants to approve your script, that is fine, but keep your honest opinions on output quality your own.
